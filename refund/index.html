Refund Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

This policy applies to “Paytick Pro” purchased in the Windows desktop app. The Android app has no paid content, so refunds don’t apply to it.

1. 7-day no-questions-asked refunds

Within 7 days of purchase, you can request a full refund for any reason. Email happymoneyeverysecond@gmail.com with your order number or the email address you used to buy.

2. How refunds are processed

Refunds are issued by Dodo Payments (the merchant of record for Paytick Pro) to your original payment method. How long it takes to arrive depends on your payment method and bank.

3. Pro after a refund

Once the refund is complete, the license key is disabled, Pro features stop on every computer, and the App returns to the free version. Your pay settings and other data are not affected.

4. After 7 days

Refunds are not available more than 7 days after purchase. If you run into a problem, contact us any time and we’ll help you fix it.

5. Your statutory rights

This policy does not affect consumer rights that cannot be excluded by law.
