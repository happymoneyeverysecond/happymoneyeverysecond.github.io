秒薪 Paytick

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 22 日

本隐私政策适用于“秒薪”（英文名 Paytick）的安卓版（包名 com.paytick）和 Windows 桌面版（以下统称“本应用”）。本应用实时显示你按工资和作息算出来的收入。我们尽量少收集数据：本应用没有账号，你填写的信息不会离开你的设备。

1. 你在本应用里填写的信息

工资、货币、工作日、上下班时间、发薪日、桌宠昵称、外观偏好等设置，只保存在你自己的设备上（安卓版在应用的本地存储里，桌面版在你电脑的用户数据文件夹里）。我们不会上传、收集或查看这些信息，也没有服务器存放它们。卸载本应用（或删除桌面版的数据文件夹）就会把这些信息一并删除。

2. 汇率查询（可选）

只有你在设置里选择“显示为”另一种货币、并把“汇率”设为“自动更新”时，本应用才会联网，向 ExchangeRate-API（open.er-api.com）请求公开的汇率数据。请求里只有货币代码（例如 NZD），不包含你的工资或任何其他设置。与任何网站通信一样，对方服务器会看到你的 IP 地址。详见 ExchangeRate-API 的条款与隐私说明。

3. 广告（仅安卓版）

安卓版使用 Google AdMob 展示横幅广告和激励广告（看完广告可以解锁角色、配色等外观）。Google 作为第三方，可能会通过其软件开发工具包（SDK）收集并处理以下数据：

  • 设备标识符，包括广告 ID；
  • 设备和应用信息（型号、系统版本、语言、应用版本等）；
  • IP 地址，以及据此推断的大致位置；
  • 广告互动和诊断数据（广告的展示、点击、加载情况及崩溃信息）。

这些数据用于展示广告、统计广告效果、防止欺诈和滥用；在获得同意的情况下，也可能用于个性化广告。数据由 Google 按照其隐私政策处理，我们看不到其中能识别你个人身份的信息。详见 Google 如何使用来自合作伙伴网站和应用的信息 和 Google 隐私权政策。

你可以这样控制广告数据：

  • 在欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士，本应用首次启动时会通过 Google 的同意管理工具征求你的同意；之后可以随时在“设置 → 广告隐私选项”里修改。
  • 在安卓系统设置 → 隐私 → 广告里，可以删除或重置广告 ID，或者选择退出个性化广告。

桌面版没有广告，不会联网收集任何广告数据。

4. 权限

  • 网络访问：用于展示广告和（开启时）查询汇率。
  • 在其他应用上层显示（悬浮窗）：仅在你开启“离开 App 时显示悬浮窗”后使用，用来在桌面显示桌宠和收入。本应用不会读取其他应用的内容。
  • 桌面版的“开机自动启动”只在你开启后，把本应用加入系统的开机启动项。

5. 我们不会做的事

我们不出售你的数据，不会把你的工资或设置分享给任何人，也不会用本应用追踪你在其他应用或网站上的活动。除上文所述的 Google AdMob 和汇率服务外，本应用不向其他第三方发送数据。

6. 儿童

本应用面向成年上班族，不针对 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

7. 数据安全与保留

你的设置只存在你的设备上，保留到你修改、清除应用数据或卸载为止。我们没有保存任何用户数据的服务器。

8. 政策更新

如果本政策有变化，我们会更新本页面和上方的生效日期。重大变化会在应用更新说明里提示。

9. 联系我们

对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请发邮件至 happymoneyeverysecond@gmail.com。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Paytick (Chinese name 秒薪), including the Android app (package com.paytick) and the Windows desktop app (together, “the App”). The App shows, in real time, how much you have earned based on your pay and schedule. We collect as little as possible: the App has no accounts, and the information you enter never leaves your device.

1. Information you enter in the App

Your pay, currency, workdays, working hours, payday, pet nicknames and appearance preferences are stored only on your own device (in the app’s local storage on Android, and in your user data folder on Windows). We do not upload, collect or have access to this information, and we have no servers that store it. Uninstalling the App (or deleting the desktop app’s data folder) deletes it.

2. Exchange rates (optional)

Only if you choose another currency under “Show amounts in” and set “Exchange rate” to “Auto” in Settings, the App connects to ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com) to fetch public exchange-rate data. The request contains only a currency code (for example, NZD), never your pay or any other setting. As with any website, their server can see your IP address. See ExchangeRate-API’s terms and privacy information.

3. Advertising (Android app only)

The Android app uses Google AdMob to show banner ads and rewarded ads (watching a rewarded ad unlocks characters, colors and other cosmetic options). As a third party, Google may collect and process the following data through its software development kit (SDK):

  • device identifiers, including the advertising ID;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version, etc.);
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • ad interaction and diagnostic data (ad impressions, clicks, loading performance and crash information).

This data is used to show ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud and abuse, and, where you have given consent, to personalize ads. Google processes it under its own privacy policy; we do not receive information that identifies you personally. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can control ad data as follows:

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google’s consent management tool when you first open it. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • In Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads.

The desktop app shows no ads and collects no advertising data.

4. Permissions

  • Network access: to show ads and, when enabled, to fetch exchange rates.
  • Display over other apps (floating window): used only after you turn on “Floating pet outside the app”, to show your pet and earnings on the home screen. The App does not read the content of other apps.
  • On Windows, “Launch at startup” only adds the App to your startup items after you turn it on.

5. What we don’t do

We do not sell your data, share your pay or settings with anyone, or use the App to track your activity across other apps or websites. Apart from Google AdMob and the exchange-rate service described above, the App sends no data to third parties.

6. Children

The App is intended for working adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data security and retention

Your settings stay on your device until you change them, clear the app’s data or uninstall the App. We do not run any servers that hold user data.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will be mentioned in the App’s release notes.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please email happymoneyeverysecond@gmail.com.
